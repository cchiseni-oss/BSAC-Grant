# Handelsbanken grant proposal: read this first

This folder holds the draft of a research project application to Handelsbanken's research foundations (Jan Wallanders och Tom Hedelius stiftelse and Tore Browaldhs stiftelse). The call opens on 1 December 2026.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Handelsbanken_Proposal_Draft.md` | The full draft. Easiest to read on GitHub. |
| `Handelsbanken_Proposal_Draft.docx` | The same text as a Word document, with basic formatting, for editing. |

The draft proposes one focused project: *The Company-State's Ledger: Taxation, Labour and Public Finance under the British South Africa Company in Northern Rhodesia, 1896–1924*. It has three research questions (public finance, tax and migration, and the change from company to Crown rule), a methods section built on the catalogue, a 36-month work plan, risk, ethics and budget sections, 36 references and an annex listing 103 prioritised archive files with Ref., Tag and Loc. numbers.

## Settled so far

- **Host:** the Economic History unit, Department of Economy and Society, School of Business, Economics and Law, University of Gothenburg, potentially with Klas Rönnbäck.
- **Archive access:** the digitised archive was accessed through the Southern African Institute for Policy and Research (SAIPAR) in Lusaka, with the right to publish from it.
- **Call:** opens 1 December 2026.

## Decisions and checks before submission

1. **Who is the main applicant.** If the foundations require the main applicant to be employed at a Swedish university, that may need to be Klas Rönnbäck, with you as co-applicant. The header table and Section 8 have placeholders for this.
2. **Self-citation.** The draft cites three of Klas Rönnbäck's BSAC publications. If he joins the application, present them as the team's previous research (Section 8 has a placeholder) and keep the bracketed phrase in Section 2.2. If he does not, delete that phrase.
3. **Closing date and grant type.** Only the opening date is known. Confirm the closing date and which grant type fits (a project grant is assumed).
4. **Limits and format.** Character or page limits for each field, required sections, language, and whether a Swedish popular science summary is required. I could not open the foundations' website or the application portal (teklafs.stanford.se), so the draft follows the usual structure of Swedish research project applications rather than the foundations' own template.
5. **Budget rules.** Maximum amount and duration, which salaries can be covered, the indirect-cost rate allowed, and whether research assistants in Zambia can be paid from the grant.
6. **Two claims that rest on catalogue descriptions.** The tax "exodus" episode (A2/1/4, Tag 307) and the contents of the 1909–10 district reports (A5/2/5 to A5/2/10) are described from the catalogue. Look at those files before you submit, because reviewers will take them as evidence the project is feasible.
7. **SAIPAR's role.** The draft names SAIPAR as the source of archive access, and marks co-organising the Lusaka workshop as unconfirmed. A short letter from SAIPAR confirming access and permission to publish would strengthen the application if the call allows attachments.

## About the references

- I checked every reference against publisher, journal or library records found by web search in October 2026. Every work cited in the text is in the reference list, and every work in the list is cited.
- Page ranges are missing for six journal entries where I could not confirm them: Arrighi (1966), Frankema (2011), Juif and Frankema (2018), Musambachime (1981), Slinn (1971) and Rönnbäck and Ngoma (2024, published online). Add them from the articles themselves. The DOIs shown were checked.

Confirm it with the Gothenburg University Library or the British Online Archives collection "Colonial Africa in Official Statistics, 1821–1953".
